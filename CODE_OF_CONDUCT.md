# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a friendly, harassment-free experience for everyone.

**Expected:** be respectful, welcome differing viewpoints, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.

**Unacceptable:** harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate. Report concerns to the maintainers by opening a private security advisory or contacting them via the email on their GitHub profile.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
